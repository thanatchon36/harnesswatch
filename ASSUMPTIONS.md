| A16 | 2026-10-15 | Black-box probes (`observe`, the bench) run in disposable containers with no vendor login. Harnesses that only work with a vendor login are observed pre-login (install plus first run) and are otherwise `N/A (no access)`. | §8.3, §8.8 and A05: the author's real credentials never enter containers. | A17 | 2026-10-15 | `observe` records only metadata: hostnames and ports (CONNECT targets), file paths, modes and sizes, process names and command lines, and a scan of the harness's own output for prompts.
